Mccalum

Context:
  - f(x, x_r) squarefree polynomial of positive degree on x_r
  - D(x) discriminant of f over x_r
  - S connected submanifold on R^(r - 1)
  - f degree invariant of nonnegative degree over S
  - D order-invariant over S


Goal:
  - f analytic delineable over S
  - each f-section over S order invariant

f-section: at a point s \in S is the set of points p \in R such that f(s, p) = 0; at a region is just the union of the points over each point of the region.
